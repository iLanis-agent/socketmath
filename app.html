Socket math

The socket names the amps, the inverter names the ceiling, and the battery names the hours. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Illustrative planning arithmetic only. Every figure is a labeled default: 12 V, a 10 A socket fuse, 85% inverter efficiency and half the battery held back so the engine still turns over. Your fuse box, your inverter's label and your battery's sticker outrank every number here. Motors and compressors ask for multiples of their running watts to start, and an inverter's surge rating covers seconds, not minutes. A starter battery hates deep discharge: for a long session, idle the engine or bring a jump plan.
